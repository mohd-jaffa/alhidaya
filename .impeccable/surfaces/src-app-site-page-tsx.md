## Scope

The public site (plan §6, §30): every public page, the site shell, sections, forms and system pages. Persuade mode. The admin panel is a separate Operate surface, shaped in R6.1.

## Audience and job

Families in Doha arranging home nursing, mostly on phones and often worried; hospital, clinic and school managers judging a staffing agency; nurses looking for work. Job: each knows within seconds what Al Hidaya does and takes their own next step (WhatsApp/Call, Request a Quote, Careers).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Al Hidaya means "the guidance"; the site is a hospital wayfinding system that tells every visitor where they are and sends them down their own coloured line. It refuses the smiling-nurse hero, icon-card grid, stats strip and testimonial carousel.

OWN-WORLD: clinical white ground; deep-blue sign panels with white type; three lines from the logo (sky = care at home, deep blue = staffing, leaf green = careers), each also named and pictogrammed; station dots, "You are here" markers, arrows; one legible signage sans at sign scale; licensed care photography framed as panels.

STORY: the visitor understands Al Hidaya brings qualified nurses home and to institutions across Doha, day and night; believes it is organised and professional; takes their line's action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a large deep-blue sign: the headline standing alone, the supporting line, a care photo panel beside it on desktop; beneath, three full-width coloured lines starting from a navy "You are here" station (plan D68, never a label above the headline), with stations and working actions (WhatsApp · Call; Request a Quote; Careers). Phone: the sign fills the width, the lines stack as tall tappable rows. Signature: the lines draw onward with the scroll through each section as a station while a marker travels.

FORM: hospital and metro bilingual wayfinding signage; first on the re-rolled ordered list (Impeccable's pick); seed key 04485a26 (re-roll 1).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Logo colours only (D35); light only (D28); GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, transform/opacity only, content visible by default, reduced-motion path (plan §30.4); performance budgets (plan §31); nothing childish; no invented proof (D7); Arabic-ready, logical properties (plan §33). Code-led build: no comps.
